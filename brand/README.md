# buti brand

Wordmark, mark, and colors come from [BartInTheField/buti](https://github.com/BartInTheField/buti/tree/main/brand).

The wordmark is `buti` in JetBrains Mono ExtraBold, lowercase. It is never capitalised.

Tagline:

> **Parallel** agentic workflow
> **Code review** your agent can act on
> On top of **GitButler**

Violet `#7c5cff` on Ink `#171717` is the lockup. The lanes are Paper `#ececec`. The commit dot is Mint `#2dd4bf`.

`mark.svg` is the app icon. At 16px the commit dot is dropped (`mark-16.svg`). `logo.svg` is the mark plus the wordmark for dark backgrounds. `logo-dark-text.svg` is the same lockup with the wordmark in Ink, for light backgrounds.
